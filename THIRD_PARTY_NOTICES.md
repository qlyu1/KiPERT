# Third-party resources

The study uses the data and prior resources listed in [datasets.tsv](data/manifests/datasets.tsv) and [priors.tsv](data/manifests/priors.tsv). The examples contain derived expression summaries, training-specific kernels and fitted maps. Upstream resource owners retain their rights. Original raw prior databases, commercial fonts, native comparator repositories and third-party model checkpoints are distributed by their respective providers.

The comparator source records identify the available commits and upstream license filenames in [baselines/third_party_repositories.json](baselines/third_party_repositories.json). The applicable permissions for specific upstream resources are determined by their providers. The authors' own rights notice is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
