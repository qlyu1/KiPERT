# Reproducibility of the manuscript results

These results were checked from the initial-submission package on a CPU. The [panel-by-panel ledger](evaluation/paper_results.tsv) gives each source and the exact scope of verification. The [reference run](evaluation/verified_run.json) contains unrounded values and timings.

| Evidence | Results verified |
|---|---|
| Model refit with frozen hyperparameters | The Tian CRISPRi split 101 tutorial reproduces all 33 × 14,581 server predictions exactly on the tested system. The Replogle exp6 fold 0 examples refit all three availability arms, each 36 × 5,000, to within 6 × 10⁻¹⁸ of the sealed predictions. These fits start from real training pseudobulk responses and training-normalized kernels. The alignment and model coefficients are fitted again. |
| Fig. 3h from a new fit through the final statistic | Fresh seen-2 predictions for 6 held-out pairs were averaged over contexts, joined by gene ID to 1,000 expression genes and compared with experimental observations. All 6,000 pair–gene prediction means match the archival float32 array exactly. KiPERT contrast MSE is 0.0011211526 versus 0.0022864153 from the archived additive predictions. Reduction is 50.9646%. |
| Inference using fitted parameters | SEH1L transfers into K562, HepG2, RPE1 and Jurkat were recomputed for 6,642 genes each. The largest difference from archived raw means was 1.34 × 10⁻¹⁵ or smaller. Source GP fitting, temperature selection and OT estimation used their archived results. The separate Fig. 4f correlations of 0.972–0.983 use stored scoring-adapted gene means and observed means. |
| Fig. 2 and Fig. 3 benchmark summaries | Recalculated KiPERT’s best or joint-best rank on 9 of 10 metrics, Adamson shared-response-corrected Pearson 0.472 versus 0.304, all three reported cosine PDS values, the seen-0/1/2 direction-error and nMSE trends, and Norman seen-0 retrieval at ranks 1/4/8. Full comparisons use the released condition-, target- or split-level scores. |
| Fig. 4g paired comparison | All 3,712 matched conditions and 1,595 distinct perturbation genes contributed. Expression-change MSE increased by 7.3829%. |

## Fig. 4g paired bootstrap interval

Run `python -m evaluation.verify --only fig4_interval` from the repository root. With seed 20261113, the check first draws within-cell-line bootstrap samples. It then resamples 1,595 perturbation genes jointly across cell lines for 5,000 draws, keeping the two methods paired. In each draw it gives the four cell lines equal weight and calculates the percentage increase as 100 × (mean KiPERT PDS / mean PMean PDS − 1). The 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles are **5.23056% and 6.56947%**, respectively. Both match the endpoints in the [November 19 manuscript record](evaluation/manuscript_version.json) at two decimal places. The same draws reproduce the archived 95% interval for the absolute PDS difference (0.0347913–0.0429562).

## Scope

The package supports offline checks of the examples, scoring summaries and source figures. The full five-dataset single-gene fitting sweep, all 60 double benchmark arms, source-GP retraining, cardiac model fitting, third-party comparators and full ablation sweeps were not rerun in this verification. Their archived source and recorded configurations are included. There is no public archive identifier yet for all full prepared arrays. A successful plot redraw confirms that the inputs and rendering code run; it confers no additional model-fitting verification.
